Network Sonifier

A working model of the Oxford study by Axon, Happa, Goldsmith & Creese. Synthetic traffic at about 4,500 packets per second is sampled, and every sampled packet header is played as notes. Inject an attack and listen for it, open a Wireshark capture, or listen to this computer's own traffic live.

Issue in the paper

DDoS and port scan confused

Both are heard as low dissonant notes, and listeners named six port scans as DDoS.

Issue in the paper

FTP false positives

Every FTP packet plays the same piano note, so an ordinary file transfer can pass for an exfiltration.

Issue in the paper

Connectivity loss is silence

The sound only thins out. It was the slowest attack to detect, at 6.4 s, and some listeners thought the test had ended.

Issue in the paper

Static hotlists

The hotlist is fixed after the first 30 s, so a harmless change in normal traffic sounds dissonant from then on.

Issue in the paper

Auditory fatigue

Every packet plays at full level all the time. The paper leaves listener fatigue untested.
